# Otium 0.1.3

- **Otium asks whether you pay for electricity.** Setup used to count electricity unless you changed it, and Quick
  start never asked. On a household price a processor rarely earns more than the power it uses, so many new
  computers sat paused. Both setups now ask "Do you pay for this computer's electricity?" with nothing preselected:
  if it's included in your rent, paid by someone else or free, Otium counts it as zero and earns whenever you're away.
- **Paused for electricity says why, with the numbers.** The overview shows what a paused processor or graphics card
  would earn a day and what its electricity would cost, with a one-click "I don't pay for electricity". Mining then
  starts at once instead of after the next 30-minute check, and a cheaper hourly price also lifts the pause right away.
- **"You've been paid."** When the pool pays your wallet, the overview says so, with the amount and how much it has
  paid you in all. It's an easy moment to tell a friend: copy a message or save a picture with your invite code, and
  see what an invite gives both of you.
