# Otium 0.1.1

- **Graphics cards mine again.** Since 0.1.0-rc.14, Otium read the graphics-card miner's progress from a line it no
  longer prints, so every card looked stalled after three minutes and was set aside for longer and longer ("Graphics
  card isn't mining: it showed no hashing for 3 minutes"). Otium now reads the miner's statistics table, and the card
  mines as before. Nothing to do on your side.
- **Computers with several graphics cards.** Each card is judged on its own: a coin runs on every card that can mine
  it instead of being blocked by the weakest one, a card short of free memory is left out on its own, and the
  graphics built into most Ryzen processors no longer counts as a mining card. Electricity from NVIDIA and AMD cards is
  added together, and every card is named.
- **More stats in Details.** Each device shows its load and temperature, and graphics cards also their fan, power,
  clock and memory in use.
- **Internet sharing shows where it stands:** whether Proxybase has your computer in its trial pool, and how many
  buyer sessions are open across Proxybase right now.
- **Your fee and invites stay fair.** Otium's fee, fee-free days and the inviter's share now come from terms the
  server signs for your computer, and how long you've mined is kept on the server too, so they can't be changed on the
  computer. An invite counts once per payout address and per computer, needs your payout address, and is verified by
  your friend's own Otium actually mining. An invite link's code is always shown for you to keep or clear, never
  applied on its own.
- **The miners are checked before every start,** not only when they're downloaded. Otium downloads them once more
  after this update to keep the checked copy.
- **Internet sharing payouts:** Otium's share is still paid separately from your conversion. The server now tells the
  app the exact amount to send, and holds new payouts while an earlier share hasn't arrived.
