# Otium 0.1.0

The first public release. Otium earns money from your computer's spare power while you're away from it, and pays you
by PayPal or crypto.

- **One button.** Three short screens show what this computer could earn, then Otium runs in the background, only while
  you're away, and pauses the moment you're back.
- **Never at a loss.** It pauses when electricity would cost more than it earns. In Norway, Sweden, Denmark and Finland
  it follows live hourly electricity prices.
- **Your balance in dollars.** Add your email to keep it safe and to use it on several computers. Cash out from $5 by
  PayPal or $2 in Monero; every cash-out shows its status in the app, and an email receipt arrives when it's paid.
- **Invite friends.** Ready-made messages for WhatsApp, Discord and email. Friends mine fee-free for 30 days and you
  get 5% of what they earn. A monthly leaderboard shows the top inviters.
- **Optional internet sharing**, only if you agree to it, paid into the same balance.
