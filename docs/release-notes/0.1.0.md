# Otium 0.1.0

The first public release. Otium mines with your idle processor and graphics card while you're away, set up and
switched for you. The pools pay you directly, in the coin you choose, at your own wallet;
Otium never holds your money.

- **Four short screens.** See what this computer could earn, choose where to be paid (USDT on TRON by default, or BTC,
  LTC, BNB, SOL, TRX, ETH or Monero), then Otium runs in the background, only while you're away, and pauses the moment
  you're back.
- **Measures your own PC.** Right after you start, a two-minute test runs the miners while you watch and shows what
  this computer really earns a month, after the fee and electricity. If the graphics card earns ten times what the
  processor would, the processor is left off (Settings turns it back on).
- **Shows what your time away was worth.** "While you were away: $0.84" when you come back, and a summary of last
  month in the first days of a new one, each ready to copy with your invite code or save as a picture.
- **Earns through the night.** While it's earning it keeps the computer awake (the screen still turns off), so it
  doesn't stop when Windows would normally put the PC to sleep. Laptops on battery are left alone.
- **Faster processor mining**, optionally: one Windows permission lets the miner use large memory pages, which makes
  it noticeably faster on most processors.
- **Never at a loss.** It pauses when electricity would cost more than it earns. In Norway, Sweden, Denmark and Finland
  it follows live hourly electricity prices.
- **Paid straight to your wallet.** unMineable pays your address daily once you reach its minimum (1.5 USDT for USDT
  on TRON), Monero included. US citizens and residents (Otium asks) are paid in Monero by MoneroOcean, since unMineable
  doesn't serve them. The overview shows how close you are to the next payout and warns you if the pool won't pay by
  itself, for example when its "Auto pay" switch is off for your address.
- **One wallet for everything.** Processor, graphics card and internet sharing all pay the single address you give,
  in the coin you chose.
- **Graphics cards mine the best-paying coin**, tested on your card first, AMD cards included. If a coin changes its
  rules so that your card's work stops counting (as Pearl's planned fork will for most cards), Otium moves the card to
  the next best coin by itself.
- **Fair, visible fee.** Otium's fee is 2% of mining time (about 29 minutes per day of mining), mined to Otium's own
  address. It pays for Otium's servers, for setting up payouts to your own wallet, and for development and support.
  Through Otium's referral code you pay unMineable 0.75% instead of its usual 1%.
- **Two ways to start.** Quick start turns on the processor, the graphics card and keeping the miner ready, and only
  asks where to pay you and whether to share anonymous earnings figures; Customize lets you choose each part.
- **Earn from your internet too**, if you tick it: Proxybase's client shares internet you aren't using, and the
  earnings are converted and paid to your own address in the coin you chose, like mining.
- **Invite friends.** Ready-made messages for WhatsApp, Discord and email, and a link that fills in your code when a
  friend installs from it. You both get 30 days without Otium's fee once your friend has mined for a day (up to 90 at a
  time), and for 6 months 25% of Otium's fee on their mining is mined to your address ("Earned from friends"). A
  monthly leaderboard shows the top inviters.
- **Signed updates.** Otium updates itself while you're away, and only installs versions signed with the developer's
  key, so a hijacked website or GitHub account can't push code to your computer.
- **Help when you need it.** Settings → Send a problem report sends what each miner is doing, with your addresses,
  keys and user name taken out, and gives you a reference to mention on Discord (discord.gg/4FtFDrz97t) or by email
  (contact@otium.lol).

Otium isn't code-signed yet, so Windows and macOS warn you once when you install it; otium.lol/download shows what
you'll see and what to do.
