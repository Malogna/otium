# Otium 0.1.2

- **A second way to share your internet: Mysterium.** Next to Proxybase, you can also share through Mysterium's
  network, which pays its MYST token on the Polygon network straight to your own address (automatically once 5 MYST
  has built up, minus Mysterium's 20% fee). It carries traffic for Mysterium's business customers only, Otium takes no
  fee from it and pays the one-time registration for you, and you can swap MYST for another coin on Relay. It has its
  own switch, explanation and daily limit, and needs no administrator permission. On Windows, Windows Security may
  flag Mysterium's node; Otium checks every download against Mysterium's published checksum and offers to allow it.
  If a test version installed Mysterium's network service, Advanced settings can remove it: it isn't needed any more.
- **Share disk space through AIOZ.** A third network, AIOZ, stores and delivers pieces of files for its customers
  using some disk space (20 GB at most, your choice) and upload bandwidth. It pays its AIOZ token, which Otium sends
  to your own 0x address on the AIOZ network once 5 AIOZ has built up; Otium takes nothing. It has its own switch and
  explanation and needs no administrator permission. AIOZ's network has many more computers than paying files right
  now, so earnings may be small or nothing for a while. Windows and Linux only for now.
- **Graphics cards say why they wait.** When there's no coin to mine, the card now says whether the coin prices are
  unavailable for a while (Otium's price source is down) or no coin pays right now, and mentions electricity only if
  you pay for it.
- **Internet sharing in one place.** The overview shows one "Internet connection" row with every network's pace and
  earnings added up; open it to see each network on its own line. Advanced settings has one "Internet sharing" card
  with a section per network, and turning sharing on, during setup or later, ticks every network at once (untick any
  you don't want).
